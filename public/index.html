Plan4Free is growing into one place for life’s occasions—from welcoming someone to celebrating milestones and saying goodbye. Start with a working planner below. The whole-life vision is not all available yet.

Celebrations & commitments

Choose a birthday, anniversary, naming / welcome, or wedding / commitment pack. Shape a plan, compare options, prepare selected guest or helper copies, work through the day, and revisit unfinished tasks afterward.

Funerals & memorials

The existing farewell workspace stays here: practical planning, words and keepsakes, ceremony preparation and afterward support.

Open the farewell workspace →

Existing saved plans, private backups and direct workspace links remain unchanged.

No sign-in needed. Saving is OFF until you choose it. These are separate browser-local workspaces; plans are not moved or merged automatically.

The link is public. Your work is not published.

The public site contains no family plans or photographs. Words, planning details and any portrait you choose are handled in your browser; the app does not upload them to a server or include them in measurement. This site records daily page openings, first planner edits or occasions created and keepsake/helper-runbook exports prepared. Only these event counts are sent: no plan content, cookies or visitor IDs. Repeat visits and bots can affect counts; exports prepared do not prove a saved file or a completed plan.

Edits disappear on reload unless you explicitly choose local saving. Saved data is unencrypted and accessible to anyone using that browser profile. Private backup files are also unencrypted—keep them private, and never share them as guest keepsakes.

Practical help, not just a checklist

Review suggested tasks, dates and arrangements. Keep comparisons and estimates explicit. Prepare reviewed, unsent supplier enquiries and selected handovers. Changes can trigger a fresh review; nothing silently becomes an accepted assignment or booking.

The vision—and what is still missing

We are building toward planning and doing across life, with more event-specific support still to build. Wedding / commitment celebration planning is available now; ceremony choices do not determine legal-marriage intent or status. Other event packs and legal-requirements guidance are not provided. No automatic supplier search, sending, booking, payment, live RSVP or shared collaboration is connected. You still carry out real-world actions yourself.

The farewell photo-room server, permanent guest QR, existing-video import, music editing and connected AI assistant are not available. Installation and offline use remain device-dependent.
