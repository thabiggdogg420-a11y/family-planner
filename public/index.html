A plan you control.

Work privately in your browser. Saving starts off. Choose local saving or download a private backup before closing if you want to return.

Already have a saved plan? Open the same address and browser you used before. Browser storage belongs to each address; plans are not automatically moved or merged.

Real-world actions stay yours. These tools prepare drafts and selected copies. No automatic sending, bookings, payments, live RSVP or shared collaboration.

Privacy, saving and current limits

The link is public. Your work is not published.

The public site contains no family plans or photographs. Words, planning details and any portrait you choose are handled in your browser; the app does not upload them to a server or include them in measurement. This site records daily page openings, first planner edits or occasions created and keepsake/helper-runbook exports prepared. Only these event counts are sent: no plan content, cookies or visitor IDs. Repeat visits and bots can affect counts; exports prepared do not prove a saved file or a completed plan.

Edits disappear on reload unless you explicitly choose local saving. Saved data is unencrypted and accessible to anyone using that browser profile. Private backup files are also unencrypted—keep them private, and never share them as guest keepsakes.

Private backups are not guest keepsakes. Review before sharing. Wedding planning does not determine legal-marriage status. Legal-requirements guidance, connected AI, permanent guest QR, hosted photo rooms, existing-video import and music editing are not available. Offline use depends on your device.
